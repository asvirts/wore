Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 7, 2026 · Wore for iPhone and iPad

Short version: Wore does not collect, transmit, rent, or sell your photos, wardrobe items, outfit history, or any other personal data. There is no account, no analytics SDK, and no advertising SDK.

1. Data we collect: none

Andrew Virts (“we”) does not collect your photos, garment details, prices, wears, or app usage from Wore.

2. Photos and background removal

Garment photos and subject cutouts stay on your iPhone or iPad. Background removal runs entirely on device using Apple’s Vision framework (VNGenerateForegroundInstanceMaskRequest). No photo is ever sent to a cloud vision model, remote API, or external server.

3. Where your wardrobe lives

Garments, outfit logs, categories, and trip packing lists are saved locally in Wore’s on-device SwiftData database and local app container. There are no remote database servers and no mandatory cloud backups.

Today’s outfit snapshot is shared between the main app and the Wore Home Screen widget on your device through an Apple App Group (group.com.asvirts.wore). That data never leaves your device.

4. Purchases

Wore Pro is processed directly by Apple through StoreKit 2. We never see or store your payment card details, billing address, or Apple Account credentials. Apple communicates only whether the non-consumable Wore Pro entitlement is active.

5. Deleting your data

You can delete individual garments, outfits, or trip lists anytime inside the app. Deleting the Wore app from your device immediately removes all local photos, cutouts, and database records from that device.

6. Children’s privacy

Wore does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

7. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, email asvirts@outlook.com.
