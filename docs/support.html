Frequently Asked Questions

How do I restore Wore Pro on a new device?

Wore Pro is a one-time purchase with Family Sharing enabled. Make sure you are signed in with your Apple Account, open Settings in Wore, and tap Restore Purchases.

How is cost per wear calculated?

Cost per wear is the item's purchase price divided by the total number of times you have worn it. It only appears after the item has been logged at least once. Items with zero wears stay in the Unworn list rather than showing a fake cost.

How does wear counting work?

Wore counts a maximum of one wear per garment per calendar day. Logging a piece in today's outfit increments its wear count by 1. Deselecting it on the same day removes that wear. Logging multiple outfits on the same day with the same item does not inflate the count.

What happens if background cutout fails on a photo?

Background removal uses Apple’s on-device Vision framework. If lighting or contrast makes instance detection unclear, Wore saves the original photo and gives you an instant crop tool so you can still add the piece cleanly in seconds.

Where are my photos stored?

All photos and cutout masks are stored locally on your device in the app container. They are never sent to external servers or cloud services.
